When your app acts for other readers, sign them in with [OAuth](/api/oauth/) instead of asking them to paste a
token. The SDK's `OAuthSession` owns their tokens from then on: it refreshes them before they expire, never spends
a refresh token twice, stores each new pair before using it, and tells you once when someone needs to sign in again.

Your app decides three things: its client id and redirect URI, the scopes it asks for, and where tokens are stored.

## Before you start

Register your app on hardcover.app under **Account → Developer apps**. Add the exact redirect URI the app will use,
such as `yourapp://oauth/callback`, and note the client id.

## 1. Create a session

Create one session when the app starts and hand it to the client. Store tokens somewhere secure: on React Native,
`secureStore` keeps them in the Keychain or Keystore. `onSignedOut` runs once if the reader signs out, revokes your
app on the website, or their sign-in expires.

## 2. Sign in

Ask only for the scopes your app uses; the reader sees them on the consent screen. `completeAuthorization` checks
the response really answers this sign-in, exchanges the code and stores the tokens.

## 3. Use the client

From here every request just works, across app launches too. If the reader has been signed out, requests fail with
`SignedOutError`, after `onSignedOut` has already run.

## 4. Sign out

Signing out revokes the tokens on Hardcover, so your app leaves the reader's authorized apps, and clears the store.
