A year in review pulls together the reader's finished reads, their books, and stats like the ones on the Stats page
at hardcover.app.

## Every book they finished

`reads.finished` returns reads finished in a date range, each with its book and edition, a page at a time.
`allPages` walks every page for you.

<SdkExample version="0.1" file="year-in-review" fn="yearInReview" />

## Fill in a read from before Hardcover

Readers often remember reading something years ago without the exact dates. Add the read with the dates they know
and how precise those are; the server writes the journal entries.

<SdkExample version="0.1" file="year-in-review" fn="logAnOldRead" />

## Stats that match the site

`@hardcover/sdk/stats` counts books, pages, listening time, ratings, formats, genres and authors the same way the
Stats page does. It's a separate entry point, so apps that don't show stats don't ship it. The reader's time zone
decides which year "this year" is.

<SdkExample version="0.1" file="stats" fn="yearInReview" />
