Two common ways books arrive in bulk: someone scans the barcodes on a shelf, or they bring a library over from
another service. Both match books exactly and never guess, and both tell you what didn't match so your app can ask.

## Scan one barcode

A barcode names one specific edition, so a scan is looked up by ISBN-13 or ISBN-10, never through search. Spaces and
hyphens are fine. If nothing matches (or it's a UPC, which Hardcover doesn't use), offer a title search instead.

<SdkExample version="0.1" file="bulk-scan-to-list" fn="scanToList" />

## Scan a whole shelf

`lists.addBooks` takes the raw scanner output. It looks every code up in one request, adds the exact editions in
batches paced to stay inside your rate limit, and returns anything it couldn't add in `notAdded`, with the reason.
Adding a book that's already on the list is harmless.

<SdkExample version="0.1" file="bulk-scan-to-list" fn="bulkScanToList" />

## Bring in books from another service

`books.findByExternalIds` looks up a whole list of another service's ids, such as Goodreads or Open Library ids from
an export, in one request. When the match names an exact edition, it's added as that edition.
