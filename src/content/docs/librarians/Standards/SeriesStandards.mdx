*This page covers standards for naming and organizing series, including multiple series with the same name, adaptations, and publisher collection edge cases.*

## Distinct Series with the Same Name
When two or more distinct series share the same name:
- Retain the published series name. Do not append `(Author Name)` to the displayed series name solely for disambiguation between shared names of distinct series.
- Where manual slug disambiguation is required, append `-AuthorName` to the slug (e.g., `series-name-jane-smith`).

Hardcover automatically generates a unique slug for newly created series using the series and author names. However, this behaviour does not apply retroactively to historical series metadata, so existing same-named series may still require manual slug disambiguation.

Where the books in a same-named series have different authors, assign the author of the book currently listed at series position `1` as the series author.

A series’ unique numerical ID appears in its edit URL (e.g., `https://hardcover.app/series/1092/edit`). Use this ID to confirm that you are selecting the correct series when multiple series share a name. Because the Series ID field can retain multiple values, check it after correcting a series association and remove any ID belonging to an incorrectly associated same-named series.

## Series Adaptations in Multiple Formats
When a series has been adapted into another format (e.g., when a manga is adapted into a light novel), create a separate series for the adapted format. Unless format-specific standards state otherwise, retain the original name and slug for the source series, and disambiguate the adapted series as follows:
  - **Source series:** Retain the existing series name (e.g., `The Summer Hikaru Died`).
  - **Adapted series name:** Append `(Format)` to the series name (e.g., `The Summer Hikaru Died (Light Novel)`).
  - **Adapted series slug:** Append `-format` to the slug (e.g., `the-summer-hikaru-died-lightnovel`)

<Aside type="note">
In specific cases where a format qualifier alone does not adequately distinguish between series, `(Author Name)` may be used as an additional series-name disambiguator (e.g., for some comics, graphic adaptations or other edge cases).
</Aside>

See the [Manga & Light Novel Standards](/librarians/standards/mangalightnovelstandards/) for the corresponding book and volume naming conventions.

## Publisher Collections (e.g., Penguin Little Black Classics)
For publisher or other collections where only a specific edition of a book is part of the series, a Hardcover series should not be created. The book may instead be added to a list.
