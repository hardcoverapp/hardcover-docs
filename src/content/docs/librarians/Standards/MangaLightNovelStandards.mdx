*This guide explains how to handle manga and light novel series on Hardcover, including naming conventions, numbering, and adaptation-specific edge cases.*

## Series Naming Conventions

<ImageComponent imagePath="/src/content/images/librarians/manga_series_naming.png" description="Example of light novel series naming on Hardcover" />

As with [book titles](/librarians/standards/bookstandards/#titles--translations), use the official English series title whenever one exists. Choose the cover edition separately for each volume with manga and light novels.

- **If an English edition exists for the volume**, use the official English series title and select an English edition as the book’s cover edition.
- **If an official English series translation of the title exists, but the volume has no English edition**, use the official English series title and select an original-language edition as the book’s cover edition.
- **If no official English series title exists**, use the original Japanese title followed by its rōmaji transliteration in square brackets. Select an original-language edition as the book’s cover edition, even if translations into other languages are available.

**Example**

- 小悪魔ティーリと救世主!? [Koakuma Teeri to Kyuuseishu!?]

If a series has an official English title, **use that title on its own.** Do not change an existing official English series title to a combined English–Japanese title. This applies whether the Japanese title is added before or after the English title, even if another book tracker or manga site uses that format.

For example, keep `Spy x Family` as the series title. Do not change it to `スパイファミリー [Spy x Family]` or `Spy x Family [スパイファミリー]`.

<Aside type="note">
Before renaming an existing series, update the title and format disambiguation, where needed, on at least one book in that series. This gives Hardcover a chance to refresh its mappings and associate the book with the correct series.

Before merging apparent duplicates, check the books linked to each series and verify their series IDs, especially if either series name lacks a format qualifier. Merge the records only if they represent the same series in the same format.

If a series slug contains a year or appears unnecessarily long, it may be possible to reclaim a shorter slug reserved from past deduplication. Report these cases to the Hardcover team for assistance.
</Aside>

### Disambiguating Light Novels from Manga

If a series exists in both manga and light novel formats, append `(Manga)` or `(Light Novel)` to the series title to distinguish the two. Use title case for the text in parentheses.

**Examples**

- Classroom of the Elite (Light Novel)
- Classroom of the Elite (Manga)

## Volume Titles and Numbering

<ImageComponent imagePath="/src/content/images/librarians/manga_series_naming_2.png" description="Example of light novel series naming on Hardcover" />

Use the following title format for manga and light novel books and their editions.

Naming conventions and disambiguation may vary from publisher to publisher or across other book data platforms. Hardcover sets its own standards to maintain consistency.

For books and all editions of manga and light novels, we follow the format:

`{Series Title} ({Disambiguation}), Vol. {num}: {Subtitle}`

Use title case for the disambiguation in parentheses. Do not include parentheses and their contents if the series does not need disambiguation, as is typically the case when the series was published in only one format in its original language.

**Examples**

- My Hero Academia (Manga), Vol. 2: Rage, You Damned Nerd
- Overlord (Light Novel), Vol. 1: The Undead King

**Shared series subtitles.** Place volume-specific subtitles after the volume number, as shown in the format guideline above. However, if *every volume shares the same subtitle as part of the series title*, keep it with the series title before the volume number.
